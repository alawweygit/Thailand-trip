Flying in from Phuket — flight is ~1h 20min. Grab an airport taxi or use Bolt/Grab to get to your hotel. Check in, freshen up, then hit the Grand Palace early afternoon before the crowds thin out.

Go higher. Akara Sky Hanuman delivers one of Bangkok's most dramatic panoramas — glass edges, golden hour glow, and cocktails that match the view. Then Sky Lab for that open-air rooftop energy. Feeling adventurous? Levels Club & Lounge for Bangkok nightclub vibes.

📸 Photo Tip — Night 2
The Bangkok skyline at night is one of the most photogenic in Asia. Shoot from rooftop edges, use portrait mode for bokeh city lights behind you, and shoot wide-angle for the full panorama. Golden hour (6–7PM) gives the best sky gradient.
🏆 BANGKOK TIPS
🚕
Use Grab everywhere. Bangkok taxis often refuse to use meters — Grab (equivalent to Uber) is fixed price, reliable, and a lifesaver. Download before landing.
👗
Dress code at temples: Grand Palace is strict — no shorts, no sleeveless. Bring a sarong or buy one outside for ~50 THB. Shoulders and knees must be covered.
💳
Cash + cards: ICONSIAM and rooftop bars take cards. Street food and markets are cash only. Withdraw THB from ATMs — there's a ~220 THB fee per withdrawal so take out larger amounts.
🌡️
Bangkok heat: May is hot and humid (~35°C). Plan outdoor activities in the morning or after 5PM. The malls and rooftops are your best friends during peak heat hours.
🛕
Book Mahanakhon early: The skywalk glass floor sells out. Book online at least a day in advance, especially for sunset slots (5–7PM).
🍜
Thipsamai timing: Go before 7PM or expect a 30–45 min wait. Worth it every time. Order the premium Pad Thai wrapped in egg — it's the one.
⛵
Chao Phraya Express Boat: Take the river boat between ICONSIAM, Grand Palace, and Chinatown. Scenic, cheap (~15 THB), and avoids traffic completely.
