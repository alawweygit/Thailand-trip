🏆 TRAVEL TIPS
🚗
Transport: Rent a scooter or use Grab (Thailand's Uber) everywhere. Taxis love to overcharge tourists — always negotiate or use the app.
💵
Cash is king: Many markets and street vendors don't take cards. Withdraw Thai Baht from ATMs on arrival — 1 OMR ≈ 95 THB roughly.
☀️
Sun protection: SPF 50 is non-negotiable. May is hot season — you will burn faster than you think, especially on boats and beaches.
📱
SIM card: Pick up a DTAC or AIS tourist SIM at the airport — 15 days unlimited data for around 300 THB. Essential for Grab and navigation.
🏨
Stay in Patong: Best location for Bangla access. Kata or Karon are quieter alternatives if you want beach vibes over nightlife proximity.
🎒
Island day packing: For Phi Phi — reef shoes, waterproof phone pouch, sea-sick meds, sunscreen, and a dry bag. Boat transfers get splashy.
🌙
Bangla timing: Things only get going after 11PM. Pre-game at your hotel or a rooftop bar first. Pace yourselves — it's a 5-night trip.
🙏
Respect Thai culture: Cover shoulders and knees at temples. Always wai (bow) back if greeted. Thais are incredibly warm — meet that energy.
